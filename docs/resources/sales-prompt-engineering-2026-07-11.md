# AI Sales Specialist Prompt Engineering — 2026-07-11

## Purpose

Generate paste-ready prompts for the browser-based internal Spectrum AI agent from minimal live-call context. The page is additive to Quick Lookup, Sales Coach, and Across the Spectrum.

## Internal selling signals retained

- Meaningful Mobile presentations include pricing, plan, feature, and benefit.
- Mobile is introduced as a real conversation, not a late-call mention.
- Internet tier discovery asks how many people and devices use the service, how they use it, which simultaneous high-demand activities occur, and what performance they expect.
- Premium Internet recommendations are gated by live serviceability and equipment rules.
- Invincible WiFi discovery asks about people, devices, dead zones, buffering, work from home, gaming, simultaneous streaming, and outage continuity.
- Device acquisition paths retain compatibility, documentation, payment-history, trade-in, activation, and current-offer checks.
- Incentive values and individual performance data are time-limited operational material and are excluded from the prompt corpus.

## Evidence stack

1. Adaptive selling and customer orientation: Franke and Park combined 155 samples covering more than 31,000 salespeople. Adaptive selling increased self-rated, manager-rated, and objective performance measures. DOI: 10.1509/jmkr.43.4.693.
2. Question asking: Huang, Yeomans, Brooks, Minson, and Gino found that asking more questions, especially follow-up questions, increases liking in conversation. DOI: 10.1037/pspi0000097.
3. Psychological reactance: Dillard and Shen modeled reactance as intertwined negative cognition and anger when persuasive language threatens choice. DOI: 10.1080/03637750500111815.
4. Motivational-interviewing conversation analysis: Shah et al. annotated 14,797 utterances across 734 conversations; reflections and affirmations predicted higher client satisfaction, while experienced counselors used more open questions. arXiv:2211.05182.

## Operational method

- Adaptive discovery selects the next question from current customer context.
- OARS supplies open questions, affirmations, reflections, and summaries.
- A-R-M handles objections: acknowledge, respond, move forward.
- Need–proof–benefit ties each verified feature to a customer-stated need.
- The assumptive close begins only after need, fit, eligibility, and complete disclosure are established, and it preserves a valid choice.

## Integrity gates

- Use current internal KBase, biller, Agent OS, approved offers, and Competitive Edge as authority.
- Never invent pricing, promotions, eligibility, speeds, coverage, compatibility, credits, deadlines, or terms.
- Disclose fees, equipment, activation, and material limitations before agreement.
- Do not manufacture scarcity, hide alternatives, diagnose customers, or target distress, age, disability, confusion, or financial vulnerability.
- Do not enter customer account identifiers or protected information into the local Study Buddy app.
